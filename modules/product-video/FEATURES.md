# Product Video: Feature Spec

- **ID**: `product-video` · **Plugin**: ShopGlut · **Code**: `modules/product-video/module.php`
- **Benchmarked against**: Product Video Gallery for WooCommerce (10k), Product Video for WooCommerce, Merchant (video module)

## Built (7 Nov)
- [x] YouTube, Vimeo and MP4 per product
- [x] Under the images, in a tab or under add to cart
- [x] Lazy-loaded, responsive

## Free: full
- [ ] Video as a slide in the product gallery, with a play icon on its thumbnail (classic gallery and ShopGlut layouts)
- [ ] Several videos per product
- [ ] Video per variation (shows when that variation is chosen)
- [ ] Click-to-load: thumbnail first, player only after a click (youtube-nocookie for privacy)
- [ ] Options: autoplay muted, loop, controls, start time
- [ ] Open in a lightbox
- [ ] VideoObject schema when SEOGlut is not active

## Pro
- [ ] Video as the first (main) gallery item
- [ ] Video on shop cards: plays on hover
- [ ] Video tab with a playlist
- [ ] TikTok, Instagram and Facebook video embeds
- [ ] Shoppable video: products linked under the video
- [ ] Bulk add a video to many products (CSV with product SKU + URL)

## Tests
- [ ] YouTube, Vimeo, MP4 on classic gallery, ShopGlut layout and block theme
- [ ] No YouTube request before a click
